Sources & Legal Authorities

All legal analyses, empirical statistics, and diagnostic critiques hosted across PseudoToolkit are grounded in peer-reviewed scientific literature, state statutes, investigative news reporting, and federal guidelines.

1. Empirical Research & Scientific Validity Studies

  • American Psychiatric Association (2022).
    Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (5th ed., text rev.) (DSM-5-TR). Official diagnostic criteria for Antisocial Personality Disorder (F60.2) and the Alternative Model for Personality Disorders psychopathic features specifier.
  • Dutton, K. (2012).
    The Wisdom of Psychopaths: What High-Flyers, Criminals, and Sub-Spies Can Teach Us About Success. Empirical study and nationwide survey data documenting the concentration of psychopathic and antisocial traits across high-stakes careers, prominently identifying the legal profession.
  • Meier, J. S., Dickson, S., O'Sullivan, C., Rosen, L., & Phillips, J. (2019).
    Child Custody Outcomes in Cases Involving Allegations of Abuse and Alienation. Final Report to the National Institute of Justice, Award No. 2015-IJ-CX-0011, George Washington University Law School.
  • Pepiton, M. B., Alvis, L. J., Allen, K., & Logid, G. (2012).
    Is Parental Alienation Disorder inherently valid? Behavioral Sciences & the Law, 30(2), 140–153. https://doi.org/10.1002/bsl.925
  • Silberg, J., & Dallam, S. (2019).
    Abusers gaining custody in family court: A catastrophic outcome for children. Journal of Child Custody, 16(2), 140–161.

2. Statutes & Legislative Authorities

  • Kansas Legislature — K.S.A. § 23-3315.
    Grandparent and Stepparent Visitation; Best Interest of Child. Authorizes visitation rights solely based on the subjective determination that non-parental contact serves the child's "best interest."
  • Kansas Legislature — K.S.A. § 23-3203.
    Custody, Residency and Parenting Time; Relevant Factors. Defines the multi-factor judicial balancing test, including the frequently exploited "friendly parent" provision.
  • United States Congress (2022).
    Violence Against Women Act Reauthorization Act of 2022 (P.L. 117-103, Division W, "Kayden’s Law"). Incentivizes evidentiary admissibility gatekeeping and bans coercive reunification camps.

3. Investigative Media, Public Records & Digital Archives

  • Ballotpedia (2012–Present).
    Keven M. P. O'Grady. Public judicial profile, career history, appointment by Governor Sam Brownback, and retention election records for the 10th District Court in Kansas. ballotpedia.org
  • Stand With Meg (2024).
    My Judge is on the Ballot: I'm Asking You to Vote NO. Documented litigant archive, public retention campaign, and case summaries regarding Johnson County District Judge Keven O'Grady. View Substack Dispatch
  • Wikipedia Contributors (2024).
    Sam Brownback: Gubernatorial Tenure. Documented administrative records, public approval rankings, legislative veto overrides, and investigative coverage regarding the Brownback administration. View Archive
  • ABC News (2018).
    Mother, stepdad of 7-year-old slain in murder-suicide hope her death will save lives. Investigative report detailing the court's failure to credit domestic violence evidence prior to Kayden Mancuso's murder. View Article
  • YouTube Video Archive (2023).
    Courtroom mechanics and procedural realities regarding unrepresented litigants in domestic matters: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jUqewhwY3lY
