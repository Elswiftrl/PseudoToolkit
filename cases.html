Archived Case Files

Forensic disciplines without empirical scientific validation have historically been treated by courts as incontrovertible proof. Below are documented cases where subjective, uncalibrated forensic testimony contributed directly to wrongful prosecutions and convictions.

Case 01: Forensic Odontology (Bite Mark Comparison)

State of Mississippi v. Kennedy Brewer (1995)

The Flawed Science: Forensic odontology relied on the assumption that human skin acts as a reliable medium for recording bite impressions, and that dentition can be matched to an individual to the exclusion of all others. Subsequent National Academy of Sciences (NAS) and PCAST evaluations concluded that bite mark analysis lacks any scientifically validated error rate.

Case Impact: Kennedy Brewer was convicted of murder and sentenced to death after an odontologist testified with "absolute certainty" that bite marks on the victim matched Brewer’s teeth. Brewer spent 15 years incarcerated, including over a decade on death row, before post-conviction DNA testing proved his innocence and identified the actual perpetrator.
